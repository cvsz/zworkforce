# 3. Deep Dive — 8 Highlighted Ingredients

## Group 1 — Brightening & Dark-Spot Appearance

### 1. Niacinamide — Vitamin B3

Niacinamide appears near the top of the supplied ingredient list.

**General cosmetic properties:**
- Supports a more even-looking skin tone
- Helps reduce the visible appearance of discoloration and dark spots
- Supports the production of important skin-barrier lipids, including ceramides
- Supports barrier function and moisture retention
- Helps improve the appearance and feel of the skin

**Formula role:** Brightening + Skin Barrier + Moisture Retention

> Claims that a finished product “reduces scars” or makes skin “less sensitive to sunlight” require finished-product evidence and cannot be established simply from the presence of niacinamide.

### 2. Ascorbic Acid — Vitamin C

Ascorbic Acid is a form of vitamin C and an antioxidant used in skincare.

**General cosmetic properties:**
- Provides antioxidant activity
- Plays a biological role in normal collagen synthesis
- Supports care for dull and uneven-looking skin
- Is associated with pathways involved in pigmentation control
- Can support a brighter-looking complexion when appropriately formulated

**Formula role:** Antioxidant + Brightening + Collagen Support

> Performance depends strongly on concentration, pH, stability, packaging and the overall finished formulation.

---

## Group 2 — Hydration & Dry-Skin Care

### 3. Tocopherol — Vitamin E

Tocopherol is an antioxidant and skin-conditioning ingredient widely used in cosmetic formulations.

**General cosmetic properties:**
- Provides antioxidant support
- Conditions the skin
- Supports moisture retention
- Complements formulations intended for dry or rough-feeling skin
- Can complement vitamin C within an antioxidant-focused formula

**Formula role:** Antioxidant + Conditioning + Moisture Support

### 4. Panthenol — Vitamin B5

Panthenol, also known as provitamin B5, is a moisturizing and skin-conditioning ingredient.

**General cosmetic properties:**
- Helps attract and retain moisture
- Supports soft and flexible-feeling skin
- Supports skin-barrier function
- Provides soothing properties
- Useful in formulas for dry, tight or uncomfortable-feeling skin

**Formula role:** Hydration + Soothing + Barrier Support

### 5. Sodium Hyaluronate

Sodium Hyaluronate is the sodium salt of Hyaluronic Acid and functions as a humectant.

**General cosmetic properties:**
- Attracts and retains water at the skin surface
- Helps skin feel hydrated and softer
- Reduces dry or tight skin feel
- Useful for rough body areas such as elbows and knees
- Can provide a temporarily plumper and smoother appearance through hydration

**Formula role:** Humectant + Hydration + Plumping Appearance

> “Deep hydration” is a marketing-style description whose precise meaning should be evaluated using finished-product hydration testing.

## Group 3 — Soothing & Skin Conditioning

### 6. Witch Hazel
**INCI: Hamamelis Virginiana Leaf Extract**

Witch Hazel extract is present in the supplied ingredient list.

**General cosmetic properties:**
- Functions as a botanical skin-conditioning ingredient
- Naturally contains constituents associated with an astringent effect
- Can contribute to a tighter, smoother skin feel
- Frequently appears in formulas designed for a fresh or less-oily skin feel

**Formula role:** Astringent Effect + Skin Conditioning

> Finished-product claims such as “tightens pores” or “reduces inflammation” require appropriate evidence. Witch Hazel may also be less suitable for some people with sensitivity to astringent ingredients.

### 7. Chamomile Extract
**INCI: Anthemis Nobilis Flower Extract**

Chamomile flower extract is commonly used in cosmetics for soothing and conditioning.

**General cosmetic properties:**
- Supports soothing skincare
- Helps promote a comfortable skin feel
- Commonly used in soothing-focused formulas
- Contains multiple naturally occurring phytochemical constituents

**Formula role:** Soothing + Skin Conditioning

> Chamomile extracts may contain constituents such as bisabolol depending on species and extraction, but the supplied ingredient list identifies only **Anthemis Nobilis Flower Extract**. It does not separately declare Bisabolol, so its amount in this finished product cannot be established from the label.

### 8. Licorice Extract
**INCI: Glycyrrhiza Glabra Root Extract**

Licorice-root extract is widely used in cosmetic formulas targeting dull and uneven-looking skin.

**General cosmetic properties:**
- Supports a more even-looking skin tone
- Commonly used in formulas targeting the appearance of discoloration
- Naturally contains multiple active constituents
- Has potential soothing and skin-conditioning functions

**Formula role:** Brightening Support + Soothing + Skin Conditioning

> Glabridin is a known constituent associated with licorice, but the ingredient declaration only states **Glycyrrhiza Glabra Root Extract**. The amount of glabridin in this product cannot be confirmed without standardized extract specifications.
