## 8 Highlighted Vitamins & Extracts

The formula highlights eight ingredients covering brightening, hydration, barrier care and soothing/conditioning functions.

### Brightening & Uneven-Tone Care

#### 1. Niacinamide — Vitamin B3

Niacinamide is widely used to support a more even-looking skin tone and reduce the visible appearance of dark spots. It also supports normal skin-barrier function and moisture retention.

**Key roles:** Brightening • Even-looking skin tone • Skin barrier support

#### 2. Ascorbic Acid — Vitamin C

An antioxidant used in skincare to address dull-looking skin and support a brighter appearance. Vitamin C also has a biological role in normal collagen synthesis.

**Key roles:** Antioxidant • Brightening • Collagen support

### Hydration & Dry-Skin Care

#### 3. Tocopherol — Vitamin E

An antioxidant and skin-conditioning ingredient that supports moisture retention and helps dry skin feel softer and more conditioned.

**Key roles:** Antioxidant • Moisture support • Skin conditioning

#### 4. Panthenol — Vitamin B5

A moisturizing and soothing ingredient that helps attract and retain water and supports comfortable, soft-feeling skin.

**Key roles:** Hydration • Soothing • Skin conditioning

#### 5. Sodium Hyaluronate

The sodium salt of hyaluronic acid. It functions as a humectant, helping attract and retain water at the skin surface for a hydrated, plumper and softer appearance.

**Key roles:** Deep hydration • Plumping appearance • Soft skin

### Soothing & Skin Conditioning

#### 6. Witch Hazel
**INCI: Hamamelis Virginiana Leaf Extract**

A botanical extract used in cosmetic formulas primarily for skin conditioning and to support a smoother skin feel and appearance.

**Key roles:** Skin conditioning • Smoother-looking skin

#### 7. Chamomile Extract
**INCI: Anthemis Nobilis Flower Extract**

A botanical ingredient commonly used in soothing and skin-conditioning formulations, supporting a comfortable skin feel.

**Key roles:** Soothing • Skin conditioning

#### 8. Licorice Extract
**INCI: Glycyrrhiza Glabra Root Extract**

Licorice-root extract is widely used in formulas targeting dullness and uneven-looking skin tone and also functions as a skin-conditioning botanical.

**Key roles:** Brightening support • Even-looking tone • Skin conditioning

## How the Highlighted Ingredients Work Together

The eight highlighted ingredients can be grouped into three complementary functions:

1. **Brightening:** Niacinamide + Ascorbic Acid + Licorice Extract
2. **Hydration & Barrier Care:** Sodium Hyaluronate + Panthenol + Tocopherol
3. **Soothing & Conditioning:** Witch Hazel + Chamomile Extract

This supports the product's **complete body-care** positioning, with an emphasis on brighter-looking, smooth, hydrated and healthy-looking body skin.

> Actual results depend on the finished formulation, ingredient concentrations, individual skin condition and consistent use. Claims such as “X12” or “brighter skin in 7 days” shown in promotional materials should be regarded as marketing claims unless supported by finished-product testing.

## Directions

Apply to the body and spread evenly over the areas requiring skincare.

For body skin exposed to sunlight, daytime use of an appropriate body sunscreen can complement a routine aimed at reducing the appearance of sun-related dullness.

## Warning

The supplied product information states that use should be discontinued and a doctor consulted if irritation occurs.

The ingredient list contains **Fragrance** and **Arachis Hypogaea (Peanut) Oil**. People who know they are sensitive to these ingredients should check the physical label and consider patch-testing before general use.
