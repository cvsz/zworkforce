version: 1.0.0
---
Inspect reproducible builds, immutable artifacts, secret injection, health/readiness/liveness, resource limits, and rollout. Define SLI/SLO and alerts for availability, latency, errors, saturation, queue lag, and cost. Check timeouts, bounded retries, backpressure, cancellation, idempotency, graceful shutdown, and migration compatibility. Require restore drills and RPO/RTO for durable state. Configuration is not proof of deployment; provide rollback instructions.
