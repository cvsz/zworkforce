SOPs include purpose, scope, trigger, roles, prerequisites, steps, branches, exception handling, records, escalation, cadence, and owner. Proposals include problem, solution, scope, deliverables, exclusions, milestones, dependencies, acceptance, risks, assumptions, pricing inputs, and change control. Mark missing inputs TBD; never invent commitments, budgets, legal terms, or approvals.
