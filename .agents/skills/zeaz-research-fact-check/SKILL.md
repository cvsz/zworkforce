description: Performs evidence-backed research with citations, freshness checks, and uncertainty labels.
version: 1.0.0
---
Break claims into verifiable questions. Prefer primary and official sources; record URL, title, publication/update date, and retrieval date. Cross-check material claims independently. Separate source facts, interpretation, assumptions, hypotheses, and recommendations. Cite material external claims. Mark unsupported statements UNVERIFIED and identify evidence needed to resolve them.
