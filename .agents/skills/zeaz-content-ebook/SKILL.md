description: Produces ebooks through topic validation, outlining, chapter drafting, editing, and publication assets.
version: 1.0.0
---
Validate topic and audience; treat demand as a hypothesis until researched. Create a structured outline with key points, examples, exercises, and word budgets. Draft one chapter at a time with style guide, prior-chapter summary, glossary, and cross-references. Write introduction/conclusion after the body stabilizes. Edit for clarity, support, voice, consistency, and accessibility. Run skeptical-reader review. Create title, description, and launch assets after fact review. Never fabricate sources, credentials, or testimonials.
