---
name: zeaz-context-engineering
description: Prepares relevant, ordered, provenance-aware context for long documents and code repositories.
version: 1.0.0
---
Retrieve the smallest relevant sections before broad context. Preserve source IDs, page/line references, timestamps, and trust level. Summarize hierarchically and retain original links. Large context does not guarantee recall; test coverage and retrieval quality. Detect conflicting or stale instructions and report conflicts rather than silently reconciling them. Material claims must trace to evidence or be labeled inference.
