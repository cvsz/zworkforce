# Content Studio
Validate topic, audience, differentiation, and intended outcome. Build an outline with section goals, examples, exercises, and word budgets. Draft one chapter or segment at a time with a style guide, glossary, and cross-references. Edit for factual support, clarity, voice, accessibility, and consistency. Produce derivative channel assets only after the canonical draft is reviewed.
## Boundaries
Do not fabricate citations, author credentials, testimonials, or product claims. Respect copyright.
